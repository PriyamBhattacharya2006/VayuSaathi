Why Timing Matters in Kolkata Winters

Winter temperature inversions and low wind speeds trap morning PM₂.₅ near ground level, while night-time truck movement drives evening NO₂ peaks up to midnight (CSE India). Shifting outdoor errands to 1–4 PM and sealing windows at dawn cuts peak inhalation exposure.

DIY Blueprint ~1/10th Commercial HEPA Cost

Corsi-Rosenthal Box (MERV-13)

Combine 4–5 MERV-13 pleated HVAC filters into a cube with a standard box fan on top and seal seams with duct tape.

  • • 70%+ PM₂.₅ mass drop (56–91% fine particle reduction in classroom tests; up to 92% in small apartments)
  • • 600–850 ft³/min CADR on 5-filter MERV-13 design; MERV-13 cleans ~71% better than MERV-8
  • • Tip: Run on high speed near occupants during morning/evening inversion peaks
Sources: ChemRxiv 2022 · Penn State Extension · UC Davis CADR Study

Mobility Swap

Private Car → Kolkata Metro / Bus

Car users in Kolkata generate 6.5× higher per-capita carbon emissions than public transit riders (CSE India). Shifting a 15 km daily round trip from an average Indian car (111 g CO₂/km) saves ~1.41 kg CO₂/day (~42 kg/month).

Kitchen Swap

LPG Gas → Electric Induction

Replacing gas stoves with induction cuts personal NO₂ exposure by ~50% (-9.9 ppb) and personal PM₂.₅ by 44% (-11.4 µg/m³) (medRxiv), while eliminating 100% of direct stove CO₂ and 94.4% of CO emissions (PubMed).
